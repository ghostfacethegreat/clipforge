# Creator Shorts — Introductory Editing Offer

> A small, fixed-scope editing service for creators who own or are authorized to use the source footage.

## The offer

**Pilot: 3 vertical clips for $25 USD.**

For one source video up to 30 minutes long, I will:

- Find and select three promising moments.
- Edit each into a vertical 9:16 short, up to 60 seconds each.
- Add readable, reviewed captions.
- Add clean framing and basic audio leveling.
- Deliver three MP4 preview/final files.
- Include one reasonable revision round per clip.

**Turnaround:** within 3 days after receiving the usable source file, written rights confirmation, and agreed payment/deposit.

This is an introductory pilot price for a limited number of first clients. It is not a promise of views, virality, followers, campaign acceptance, or income.

## What the client supplies

1. A video file or source link they own or are authorized to provide for editing.
2. The preferred platform(s): TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or other agreed destination.
3. Correct names, spellings, and any context that must be preserved.
4. Branding assets and caption preferences, if needed.
5. Written confirmation that they have permission to authorize the edit and that any included music, footage, images, logos, and appearances may be used for the intended clips.

A publicly accessible video is not automatically licensed for reuse. I will not edit or redistribute a third-party creator's content without permission.

## What is not included

- Posting, account access, or social-media management.
- Paid ads, guaranteed reach, or engagement manipulation.
- Music licensing or clearance for material the client does not have rights to use.
- Long-form editing, complex motion graphics, custom animation, or unlimited revisions.
- Campaign applications, reward submissions, or guaranteed campaign payouts.

## How an order works

1. Client shares the source and requested style.
2. I check that the source is usable and that rights authorization is clear.
3. We agree in writing on scope, delivery date, payment method, and cancellation/refund terms.
4. Work begins after the agreed payment or deposit is received through a permitted payment method.
5. I send a private preview, apply the included revision, and deliver the final MP4 files.
6. I record delivery and payment status; I do not mark an invoice paid until funds are actually received.

I do not ask for social-media passwords, bank details in chat, or identity documents.

## Short message to a potential client

You keep control of posting, and I won't reuse your footage. If you'd like, send a video you own or are authorized to share and the style you're aiming for, and I'll confirm whether it fits the package.

Personalize this for one creator who is actively asking for editing help or whose content would clearly benefit from short clips. Do not mass-message people or claim you have watched footage you have not reviewed.
